Privacy Policy

App: UnNest:Red | Publisher: Avika Labs | Effective Date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the UnNest:Red Android application, developed and published by Avika Labs. We value your privacy and believe in total transparency.

1. No Data Collection, Storage, or Sharing

UnNest:Red does not collect, harvest, store, or share any personal data, usage data, device identifiers, or telemetric information of any kind. What is on your device remains entirely yours.

2. No Internet Access

UnNest:Red does not request or possess the Android android.permission.INTERNET permission. The app is physically incapable of connecting to the internet or transmitting information to external servers or third parties.

3. No Analytics, Advertising, or Third-Party SDKs

The application contains no analytics tracking tools, crash reporting libraries that transmit data over networks, advertising frameworks, or third-party marketing SDKs.

4. On-Device File Processing & Folder Access

All file operations occur strictly locally on your device:

  • Explicit Selection: UnNest:Red accesses files exclusively within folders you explicitly choose through Android\'s system Storage Access Framework (SAF) folder picker.
  • Source Folder: Read-only access is requested for the source directory you select.
  • Destination Folder: Read and write access is used solely to write your copied files or .ZIP archive into the destination directory you select.
  • Non-Destructive Processing: The app copies files and never deletes or modifies original files.

5. Revoking Folder Access

Folder permissions granted via Android\'s folder picker can be revoked at any time by clearing the application\'s storage/cache in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.

6. Children\'s Privacy

UnNest:Red does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because no personal information is collected, our app is completely safe for users of all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make updates or revisions to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted directly on this page with a revised effective date.
